MODELING TARGET DECISION
==================================================

Chosen target column: next_weight

Reason:
next_weight was chosen over weight_gain because:
1. It gives an absolute target value directly interpretable by farmers.
2. weight_gain between consecutive aggregation windows is a very small delta
   relative to the absolute weight, making it a noisier and harder signal to learn.
3. Predicting the next absolute weight is standard in fish growth modeling and
   enables direct comparisons with known fish growth curves.
4. Consecutive duplicate Weight rows in raw data: 2 (0.3%).
   After aggregation into time windows, each window has a distinct mean Weight,
   so predicting the next window's weight is a well-defined regression problem.
