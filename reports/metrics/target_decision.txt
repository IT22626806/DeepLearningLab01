MODELING TARGET DECISION
==================================================

Chosen target column: next_weight

Reason:
next_weight was chosen over weight_gain because:
1. It gives an absolute target value directly interpretable by farmers.
2. weight_gain is often near zero or slightly positive between consecutive readings,
   making it harder to learn a meaningful signal.
3. Predicting the next absolute weight is standard in fish growth modeling.
4. Consecutive duplicate Weight rows: 2 (0.3%),
   confirming that raw Weight repeats frequently — shifting forward gives more
   meaningful variation as the prediction target.
